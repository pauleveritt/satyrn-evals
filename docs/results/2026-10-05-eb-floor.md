# EB: does the Engine cost the same as bare Pi on tasks both pass? — no, at 23a0ef6

**Result page, 2026-10-05** (EB postponed 2026-10-06). This is the floor result that closes Phase EB's secondary, ruled 2026-10-05 ("Adopt all seven"; ledger, "2026-10-05 — EB after EB2: seven rulings", item 3). The evidence pages are [`evidence/2026-10-05-eb-cell-read/README.md`](../../evidence/2026-10-05-eb-cell-read/README.md) (the cells) and [`evidence/2026-10-05-eb2-estimates/README.md`](../../evidence/2026-10-05-eb2-estimates/README.md) (the offline read). These are development cells, and no pre-registered rule decides this page. It is a stated negative on the floor secondary, not a deciding outcome.

## The question

Release two's declared floor-parity secondary asked: on tasks both arms pass, does the Engine spend the same output tokens as bare Pi? Phase EB re-asked it on the confinement harness, at the current Engine pin (`23a0ef6`), on the two floor tasks ruled for it, guard-prefixes and review-script (ledger, 2026-10-05).

## What ran

Twelve cells per arm per task: 48 cells on Apple M5 Max, Ornith 1.5 9B on oMLX, under `confinement: extension`. Settings were EB0's: 48,000 tokens and 72 turns, the line declared at 32,000 and 48, k = 3, a 4,800 s backstop. Six frozen records: `records/2026-10-04-eb-s1-engine-*` and `records/2026-10-04-eb-s2-{baseline,engine}-*`, with results `64d95d1`, `8b12cc2`, `84ed348`, `e11efa0` and `a2ef909`. All six sittings completed, with 0 replaced and 0 infrastructure cells, and no wall-clock cut. Every Engine cell was admitted, as were 9 of 12 guard-prefixes Baseline cells and 12 of 12 review-script Baseline cells. The three un-admitted Baseline cells are confinement refusing writes to `/tmp`.

## The decider

The decider is **the arm's total output tokens divided by its delivered passes**, with the delivery count and its own test beside it. The median over delivered passes is reported as the conditional figure ("given a pass, what did it cost"). This reading was ruled 2026-10-05 (ledger item 2; spec §7), after both readings were already in committed files.

## The answer

| task | arm | cells | delivered | total tokens ÷ delivered | median per delivered pass |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| guard-prefixes | Engine | 12 | 9 | 38,497 | 28,463 |
| guard-prefixes | Baseline | 12 | 8 | 23,888 | 5,510 |
| review-script | Engine | 12 | 6 | 24,847 | 10,828 |
| review-script | Baseline | 12 | 11 | 13,180 | 10,970 |

- **guard-prefixes:** the Engine spends more per delivered pass on both readings. Total over delivered is 38,497 against 23,888. The medians are 28,463 against 5,510, and the Engine is higher in 66 of 72 cross-arm pass pairs (one-sided rank-sum p = 0.0012). Delivery is level: 9 of 12 against 8 of 12.
- **review-script:** total over delivered is 24,847 against 13,180. The medians are level (10,828 against 10,970, rank-sum p = 0.5582). The difference is delivery: 6 of 12 against 11 of 12, one-sided Fisher exact p = 0.034.

**At engine `23a0ef6` the Engine is not at floor parity with bare Pi on either floor task.** On guard-prefixes it costs more per pass. On review-script it costs about the same per pass and delivers half as often, so it costs more per delivered pass.

## Why this is stated, not certified

Section 4's parity rule cannot certify parity at a margin of 1.25 on guard-prefixes at 24 cells or fewer. Guard-prefixes' Baseline is bimodal: five passes at 2.3k–5.8k tokens, three at 16k–20k, and three cells ran to the 72-turn budget. At the delivered counts the rule declares parity 0.655–0.720 of the time for an Engine truly equal to Baseline, and 0.188–0.354 for one at 2× (EB2 README §3). A certification would need far more cells than the floor secondary is worth. So EB3 as conceived does not run (ledger item 3), and this page states the negative the cells already show.

## What the cells say about why (descriptive)

- **guard-prefixes.** The Engine never takes the cheap path that 5 of 12 Baseline cells take: read, one edit, a quick check, stop. Its contract names a new test file and the carried tests, and every Engine pass runs at least one full-suite `self_test`. In 7 of 9 Engine passes the model writes `tests/test_guard.py` (EB2 README §2). Runaway inline probing happens on both arms, so it is not the difference.
- **review-script.** The Engine's non-deliveries are three hidden-test fails, two stray-file `unavailable` cells and one `NO_PATCH`. In the `NO_PATCH` cell the model followed the contract's "Record and commit" step and the Engine discarded the candidate (ledger item 7).
- **No remedy is estimated.** Every candidate remedy changes the Engine's calls from the first turn, so offline estimation cannot discriminate here (EB2 README §6). The light path, a lighter contract for one-symbol changes, is a product decision. If a remedy is built, it is measured Engine against Engine on guard-prefixes (ledger item 6).
- **EB is postponed, not finished** (ledger, "EB postponed", 2026-10-06). The light path was declined for now. The other candidates were neither refuted nor measured: contract test lines (central estimate 16,120 tokens per pass on guard-prefixes against a required cut of 21,576), edit-shape hoisting (2,679), and self-test trimming (wall clock only). The review-script `NO_PATCH` cell's cause, the Engine discarding a model commit, is fixed at engine `9aecfb5`; these `23a0ef6` cells are not re-read.

## Limits

- These are development cells, never a deciding read, and they are not pooled with EB0's `1869397` cells or with any census.
- Delivered pass means the harness verdict `pass` at the 48,000-token budget, not pass at the 32,000-token line. The line readings are in the cell read's `grade-line` reports.
- `unavailable` verdicts (stray files, and one collection error, the `docs/lessons.md` defect) count as not delivered.
- Admission is arm-asymmetric on `/tmp` scratch writes. Two of guard-prefixes' Baseline passes are un-admitted. Excluding them moves Baseline's median to 4,052, which widens the gap.
- The 2026-10-05 review that informed the rulings is not committed. Every figure on this page is in a committed file or is recomputed below.
