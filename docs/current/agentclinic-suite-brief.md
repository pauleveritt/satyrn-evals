# AgentClinic: one useful addition to the suite

## Goal

Extend the accepted instrument by one useful AgentClinic task-condition,
verify its live route with the real engine, run a small matched screen of that
condition against Baseline, and decide what concrete development work the
retained evidence warrants. AgentClinic supplies meaningful work; observed
failures prioritize engine repairs; Evals establishes what changed.

The comparison is a planned stage of this slice, not an optional afterthought.
It is also small on purpose: four attempts observe, they do not establish a
superiority claim.

This was the proposed next sequence, not a standing authorization. **Status
2026-09-08:** stage 1 is confirmed landed; stage 2 is complete, with
`agentclinic-repair-misleading-locus` qualified at `R3` offline; stages 3 and 4
ran on 2026-09-08 under the frozen conditions in
[the pre-run record](misleading-locus-r3-pre-run-record.md); the route is
verified and the screen returned 2/2 in each arm, detecting no outcome
difference and supporting no general comparison conclusion. The sequence below is therefore complete, and the decision it asks
for is recorded with the batch. Nothing here
authorizes a merge to an external repository, a commit, or any spending beyond
those two stages. The earlier engine comparison and post-edit region candidate
stay paused. This brief does not restart them.

## Starting point

Keep the qualified `agentclinic-repair-depth-3` / `R3` condition, its three
offline witnesses, and the demonstrated live execution and offline regrade
route. Preserve the [first-milestone design](first-milestone-design.md) and
[whole-attempt deadline](whole-attempt-deadline-design.md) guarantees.

The next gap is useful task coverage, not another adjustable engine constant.
Passing fixtures establishes software behavior; one live attempt establishes
an observation. Neither establishes superiority over Baseline or a lasting
task difficulty band.

## Sequence

### 1. Finish the existing engine repairs

Check current branch and review state first; do not redo a landed repair.
Close safe truncation and the breaker's post-edit staleness repair before
freezing a live engine revision. The latter must permit useful read-back and
retesting after a change without letting edits to B reset repeat protection
for unchanged reads of A. Preserve unchanged-loop and edit/revert checks.

Use deterministic tests, not model runs, to settle these defects. State which
execution routes are supported; the Satyrn-mutator fix does not imply support
for Pi's builtin editor. Merge only with external-repository authorization.
Offline task inspection can proceed while landing is pending.

### 2. Select and qualify one additional existing task-condition

Inspect the bundled AgentClinic tasks and choose one that exercises a useful
repair behavior the accepted condition does not already cover well. Explain
the behavior, its practical value, and the additional coverage in a short
task-local note. Make this selection within the work, not through a new user
brainstorming round.

The task earns its place through useful coverage. **Separating the arms is not
a selection criterion.** Historical outcomes may inform feasibility and
budgets, but do not select a task because Engine previously won, because its
pass rate is convenient, or because a desired metric moves there. Do not
promise a middle-band score. If no existing task contributes distinct value,
report that finding rather than inventing a task, rung, or experiment
automatically.

Then establish that the condition is fair and observable. Map accessible
requirements to public feedback and hidden checks. Ordinary code reasoning is
allowed; hidden requirements are not. Public feedback need not reveal every
answer, but its scope must not mislead the solver about what success requires.

Reuse the existing qualification seam and base, known-good, and deliberately
incomplete witnesses. Run the real public and hidden checks offline. Correct
only demonstrated task defects needed for this condition; a changed prompt
or contract is a new condition, not a silent repair of historical evidence.
Do not create a general qualification platform or restore R1c by default.

### 3. Verify the live route for the selected condition

Reuse the existing attempt, preservation, grading, and offline regrade paths.
Before spending, record the exact task-condition, model, engine revision,
effective tools, environment, output location, limits, and stopping rules.
Derive practical limits from relevant retained attempts; useful feedback within
10–15 minutes is a target, not permission to truncate known legitimate work.

Request explicit authorization for this verification attempt and any
inference-bearing preflight or warm-up. GPU/server availability is needed here,
not for the offline work. Stop on infrastructure failure and retain the
evidence. This stage establishes that the condition executes and regrades end
to end; an ordinary failed repair is still a valid verification of the route.
Neither failure nor success authorizes a retry or another task.

**This attempt stays outside the stage-4 denominator.** It is retained and
reported as route verification, never pooled with the screen's cells — its
purpose, and often its conditions, differ from theirs.

### 4. Run a matched four-attempt screen

After a separate budget authorization, run **two interleaved attempts per arm**
— Engine and Baseline — on the selected condition, with the arm order recorded
before the run. Freeze everything below before the first cell.

**Match the shared settings:** task-condition, model, limits, environment, and
stopping rules are identical across arms.

**Freeze each arm's effective tools and semantics separately, and record them
per arm.** This is a product comparison, and the arms' tool surfaces may differ
by design; the point is that each surface is fixed and written down before the
run, not that the two are made the same. **No component attribution follows
from the result** — a difference between two product surfaces does not name
which part of either produced it.

The screen's denominator is the four screen cells. The stage-3 verification
attempt is not one of them.

Four attempts are a screen. They give initial comparative observations and a
second reading of the live route; they do not support a superiority claim, a
rate, or a mechanism attribution, and no result from them authorizes extending
`n`, adding a rung, or adding a task. Infrastructure failure stops the
remaining launches; an ordinary failed repair remains a counted observation.
Report planned, launched, completed, and missing attempts by arm.

### 5. Read the evidence and decide

Verify identities and expected hook evidence, retain each patch and transcript,
and regrade offline without changing the original artifacts. Use behavior
outcomes, monotonic total time, and available terminal-per-response usage;
leave lifecycle phases unmeasured rather than estimating them from filesystem
timestamps. Report counts by arm with their denominators, never a pooled rate.

Write one concise decision covering two questions, separately:

- **Retain the condition?** Adopt it for the suite, identify a specific defect
  requiring correction, or reject it with a reason. A passing run can justify
  useful regression coverage; a failing run need not be an engine defect. Do
  not tune difficulty simply to obtain a failure.
- **Does any observed difference warrant further investigation?** Name what
  was observed and what would be needed to test it. A difference at four
  attempts is a lead, not a finding, and no lead is automatically pursued: a
  further stage is a separately authorized matched screen under the existing
  comparison policy, argued from a concrete development need.

Only propose an engine repair when the observation identifies a concrete
obstruction. Preserve its deterministic component as a cheap regression test.

## Done and out of scope

This slice ends with a reviewed decision about one additional task-condition,
supported by its offline witnesses, its verified live route, and the retained
evidence of a four-attempt matched screen. Rejection is valid; unavailable
infrastructure or unfinished qualification is a blocker, not completion. No
suitable existing task is a selection finding to bring back, not a reason to
claim that the live stages ran. A completed screen whose reading is
inconclusive also satisfies this slice.

Use Terra for implementation, Sol for iterative review and recommendations,
and Astra for final acceptance. Continue routine work within authorization;
escalate external-repository authority, live spending, or material scope changes.
Use focused checks for the changed behavior and marked integrations for real
processes; do not repeat the accepted instrument project.

Exclude open-ended pathology audits, a multi-task/all-rung matrix, speculative
reporting or telemetry, new call-budget machinery, and broad Engine superiority
claims. The four-attempt screen is bounded: one condition, existing machinery,
and no automatic confirmation campaign that follows from its result. Expand the
suite again only when the next task adds useful coverage.
