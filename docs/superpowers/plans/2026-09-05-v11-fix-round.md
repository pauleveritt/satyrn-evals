## Goal

Before any budgeted V11c or V12 cell, make every model-visible contract true
about the workspace and acceptance suite, prevent attempt runtime residue and
ambient evals virtual-environment leakage, measure Engine loop-break telemetry,
and reject a batch whose transcript names a model other than the pinned one.

## Non-goals

- No V12 driver, R0/R2 authoring, creation-capable capture, or model canary.
  Those are V12 entry gates and need V12's separate plan.
- No Engine repair here. The Engine loop termination and SIGTERM spool publish
  belong to `satyrn-engine`; this tree only gains V10 recognition of its
  telemetry.
- No budgeted cell, preflight, or Engine re-smoke before the correction tree
  is reviewed, landed, and the relevant default/integration gates are green.
- No `ruff format`: it is not this project's gate and would rewrite recorded
  fixture evidence.
